DEMO HR POLICY — fictional practice data only, not KS&EW policy.
Version 1.0. Effective 1 September 2026. Applies to permanent employees unless stated otherwise.

ANNUAL LEAVE
Permanent employees receive 20 working days of annual leave per calendar year. Leave requires prior line-manager approval. Contract employees must refer to their signed contract; this handbook does not set their annual leave entitlement.

SICK LEAVE
Permanent employees receive 10 working days of sick leave per calendar year. For more than two consecutive working days, a medical certificate is required.

PROBATION
New permanent employees have a three-month probation period. Confirmation requires written HR notification following manager review.

WORKING HOURS
Normal office hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, with a 30-minute lunch break.

OVERTIME
Overtime must be approved in writing by the department head before work begins. This policy does not specify overtime payment rates or eligibility categories.

CONTACT
For exceptions or missing rules, contact the HR department. Individual leave balances are held separately and cannot be determined from this policy.
